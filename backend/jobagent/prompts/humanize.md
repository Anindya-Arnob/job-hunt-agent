Write like the candidate wrote it, not like an AI (the "human" writing rules, short form):
- Plain, specific verbs: built, trained, cut, wrote, shipped, tested. Not "leveraged", "spearheaded", "utilized", "harnessed", "orchestrated".
- No hype or filler: "passionate", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "robust", "dynamic", "innovative", "synergy", "delve", "tapestry", "landscape", "journey", "fast-paced", "game-changer".
- No em dashes. No chains of three adjectives. No "not only ... but also".
- Name real things: the tools, datasets, libraries, project names and numbers from the CV.
- Vary sentence length and how sentences and bullets start.
- Never state a lesson or a moral ("demonstrating my ability to", "this taught me", "showcasing").
- In a letter: no opener like "I am excited to apply" or "I am writing to express my interest". Start with something concrete about the role or the work.
